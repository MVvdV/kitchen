# Blueprint rollout record

- **Decision and owner** — What changed, who owns the rollout, and whether it is a shared change or a project-specific setup.
- **Source evidence** — Link the issue, run, or existing configuration that explains the change.
- **Approval and scope** — Link the recorded approval and list the covered actions and projects.
- **Blueprint revision** — Record the exact Git revision and any project-level variations.

**Follow-up** — Record what to observe on the next applicable run, the evidence when available, and the decision to keep, revise, or roll back.
