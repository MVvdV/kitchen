# Publish Delivery Tickets

Confirm that you own Plan Delivery and that the issue records an approved spec. Follow the Kitchen workflow and supported Multica CLI operations. A self-contained issue goes to Todo without a child.

For a split, reconcile existing children using stable plan keys. Create missing children as staged native sub-issues in Backlog with their criteria and dependency IDs. Record the mappings before dispatch. Keep the parent assigned to Delivery Lead in In Delivery. Move eligible first-wave children to Todo and leave later waves parked.

On a native stage wakeup, verify actual success and dependencies before promoting later work. A cancelled required child needs an explicit decision. Skip existing dispatches and stop if ownership or the workflow step changed.
