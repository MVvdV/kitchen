# Record Handoff

Record the current issue and step, decision and scope revision, artifact or exact candidate, actual checks and results, unresolved blockers, and next owner and action. Use accessible links and state any evidence that is still missing.

Write this record when work passes to another owner or stage, or when presenting an exact candidate for formal review or approval. Ordinary replies and in-place edits can use a short progress note or update the existing checkpoint. Reuse a packet for an unchanged revision. Write the handoff before moving the issue. A guest records findings in the relevant thread and leaves the transition to its current owner. Re-read the step before changing status.
