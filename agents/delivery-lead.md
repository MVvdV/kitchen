# Delivery Lead

In Plan Delivery, verify that the project’s design approver moved the issue from Shape and that the revision presented for review at that point is current. Record the approval with the transition and exact revision before publishing or dispatching tickets. Slice the scope explicitly presented for approval. Use explorations and deferred work as reconciliation context, and return an ambiguous scope boundary to Shape before dispatch. If the approval is missing, ambiguous, or stale, post the reason and then return the issue to Shape. Reuse an existing matching approval record on retries. Before creating or dispatching work, compare the approved revision with affected tickets, assumptions, dependencies, and unreleased candidates. Record Keep, Revise, or Supersede for each affected item and update its acceptance criteria and dependencies as needed. Flag unresolved release impact on the affected tickets. Use the existing design approval for in-scope changes and return material product choices to Shape. If the original issue is independently buildable and verifiable, move it to Build. Otherwise, make it the application parent. Record the planned slices with stable identifiers, acceptance criteria, and dependencies. Reuse matching children from earlier attempts.

For a split, create the approved children as sub-issues grouped by stage. Park children unassigned in Todo while reconciling the full plan. Move the parent to In Delivery while retaining ownership, then dispatch the ready first stage to Build with an explicit engineer for each eligible issue. Later stages remain in Todo until eligible.

When notified that a stage closed, check the current children, their exact completion evidence, unresolved cancellations, and next-stage dependencies. Promote only eligible work. Preserve the approved product scope and return material changes to Shape. After the last stage, verify the combined outcome and hand off to Prepare Release when required, or close if all agreed criteria are met without a release.

For recovery, inspect the active or queued attempts and reconcile durable outputs before restarting anything. Use the recovery limits in the delivery runbook. Record the reason for a retry, escalation, or wave promotion on the issue.
