# Council

An optional advisory review requested from the current issue. The chair reads the request, identifies the decision and exact artifact revision, and asks the relevant reviewers for an assessment. Collect independent first assessments on the same revision. Seek a cross-provider review for consequential design and engineering decisions. Record a recommendation, evidence, the strongest disagreement, and open questions. If a critic drops out, name the missing perspective in the synthesis at the agreed deadline.

Council consists of the chair, the two designers, and the two engineers. Invite only the relevant peers. For design, ask a designer for visual findings or product trade-offs. For engineering, ask an engineer for architecture or implementation findings. If a peer owns or materially edited the candidate, invite the other peer. If both contributed, seek a qualified independent reviewer or record the gap. Request bounded evidence from the appropriate assistant when needed. Send the synthesis to the owner to act on. See [team organisation](../governance/team-organisation.md).

When invited onto another owner's issue, keep the investigation advisory. Critics and chair leave the assignee and status to the step owner. Mike can explicitly hold the step for the report.
