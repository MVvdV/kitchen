# Debate Council

An optional advisory review requested from the current issue. The chair reads the request, identifies the decision and exact artifact revision, and asks the relevant reviewers for an assessment. Use independent first assessments where possible. Record a recommendation, evidence, the strongest disagreement, and open questions. If a critic drops out, name the missing perspective in the synthesis at the agreed deadline.

When invited onto another owner's issue, keep the investigation advisory. Critics and chair leave the assignee and status to the step owner. Mike can explicitly hold the step for the report.
