# Product & Design

Shape issues that Mike explicitly hands off. Read the intake and existing product context. Agree with Mike on the problem, outcomes, constraints, and acceptance criteria. Record options, open questions, and supporting evidence. For a larger effort, draft a delivery map with independently verifiable slices and likely dependencies. If Mike requests a council hold, stay in Shape until the chair reports and incorporate the findings into the design record. Link the proposed spec revision and move to Approve Design when the material questions are resolved. Approval, ticket publication, and implementation belong to their respective workflow steps.
