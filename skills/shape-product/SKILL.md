# Shape Product

Read the brief and available product evidence. Describe the desired outcome and constraints in the user's terms. Ask about unresolved choices only when the answer changes scope, behavior, or verification. Explain the consequence of each choice and offer a recommendation.

During discussion and routine edits, refer to the live design and reply with the change or recommendation and the decision needed now. Default to zero attachments. Include a single focused visual only when the specific question needs it and the live page or frame is insufficient. Save durable decisions in the existing working notes.

When a question has its own outcome and can be shaped and reviewed separately, search related Shape and Todo issues first. Reuse relevant work or create a linked, unassigned Todo follow-up with the inherited decisions, scope, draft acceptance, and dependencies. Link the source and follow-up both ways. Wait for the authorized project starter to dispatch it unless the project explicitly grants autonomous Shape dispatch. Record impacts in both directions when shared assumptions change. Update parked drafts for settled decisions and leave active design changes to their owners. Hold approval of any affected design while a material dependency is unresolved.

In the handoff brief, record the problem, intended user, scope and exclusions, decisions, open questions, and observable acceptance examples. For a large effort, include a draft sequence of outcomes and dependencies. Keep proposals distinct from approved decisions. When the material choices are settled and the candidate is ready for approval, the Shape owner publishes one documented, immutable revision and marks it ready in the issue. Do this without waiting for a request. Freeze a candidate for an explicitly requested independent review or ownership transfer only when the recipient needs a pinned artifact. Keep routine edits in the working design, even when the changes are substantial. Reuse a matching packet. If a material edit changes a published candidate, mark it superseded and publish the replacement only when ready again.

When an issue names a design tool, use its tooling skill for canvas work. Preserve an accessible, tool-independent preview and decision notes with the review revision so later roles can verify what was approved.
