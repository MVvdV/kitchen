# Shape Product

Read the brief and available product evidence. Describe the desired outcome and constraints in the user's terms. Ask about unresolved choices only when the answer changes scope, behavior, or verification. Explain the consequence of each choice and offer a recommendation.

During discussion, keep replies to the answer, the change or recommendation, and any decision needed now. Use the live design when available. Include a single focused visual when a specific visual detail materially helps resolve the question. Save accepted decisions in the existing working notes when they need to survive the conversation.

In the handoff brief, record the problem, intended user, scope and exclusions, decisions, open questions, and observable acceptance examples. For a large effort, include a draft sequence of outcomes and dependencies. Keep proposals distinct from approved decisions. When the material choices are settled and the candidate is ready for approval, the Shape owner publishes one documented, immutable revision and marks it ready in the issue. Do this without waiting for a request. Also freeze a candidate when explicitly handing it to a reviewer or new owner who needs a pinned revision. Reuse a matching packet. If a material edit changes a published candidate, mark it superseded and publish the replacement only when ready again.

When an issue names a design tool, use its tooling skill for canvas work. Preserve an accessible, tool-independent preview and decision notes with the review revision so later roles can verify what was approved.
