# Shape Product

Read the brief and available product evidence. Describe the desired outcome and constraints in the user's terms. Ask about unresolved choices only when the answer changes scope, behavior, or verification. Explain the consequence of each choice and offer a recommendation.

Return the problem, intended user, scope and exclusions, decisions, open questions, and observable acceptance examples. For a large effort, include a draft sequence of outcomes and dependencies. Keep proposals distinct from approved decisions. When ready for review, identify one current brief or prototype revision and link to an immutable copy so a later approval can be traced to what the reviewer saw.
