# Slice Delivery

Read the approved outcome, exact artifact revision, and acceptance criteria. Surface missing, ambiguous, or stale approval before treating proposed scope as ready for delivery. Slice the scope explicitly presented for approval. Use explorations and deferred work to identify dependencies, and keep them out of dispatch unless the approved scope includes them.

Before creating or dispatching work, compare the approved scope with affected existing tickets, dependencies, assumptions, and unreleased candidates. Record Keep, Revise, or Supersede for each affected item, with its exact version, evidence, downstream acceptance changes, and next step. Record the scope checked if there is no impact. Hold affected unreleased candidates while impact is unresolved. Reuse the approval for changes inside the approved scope and surface material product choices for human decision.

Keep a self-contained change as one ticket. Otherwise identify the smallest end-to-end outcomes that can be implemented, integrated, and verified independently. Split by observable behavior where possible.

For each slice, specify a stable key, outcome, acceptance evidence, prerequisites, and wave. Start a later wave when there is a real dependency or an outcome that needs review first. Keep unresolved scope choices visible. Return the plan for execution by the caller.
